The Tasks page has been completely redesigned to give you more clarity and control over your prior authorization workflow.

## List and Board views

Switch between a list view and a board view using the toggle in the top-right corner of the Tasks page. The board view organizes tasks into columns by category, while the list view presents them in a compact table with collapsible category sections.

<Tip>
  The board view is the default because it gives you a quick snapshot of all
  your tasks without excessive scrolling.
</Tip>

## Category grouping

Tasks within each view — Not Supported, Ongoing, Action Required, and Complete — are now organized into categories so you can quickly find what needs your attention.

### Ongoing categories

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>In Progress</strong> — Akomi agents are currently working on the
    task. This can take anywhere from a few minutes to an hour.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Pended by Payer</strong> — The payer has pended the request. We
    check the portal and fax periodically throughout the day and update the
    status.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Appeal in Progress</strong> — An appeal has been submitted. We check
    the portal and fax periodically throughout the day and update the status.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Under Akomi Review</strong> — Tasks that require additional
    attention from Akomi's team for quality assurance or exception handling.
    Turnaround time is 1 day.
  </li>
</ul>

### Action Required categories

<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Audit Inquiry</strong> — Akomi's clinical audit flagged an issue
    such as a missing ICD code, an information request, or an LMN that needs
    approval.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Blocked Needs Attention</strong> — Something is preventing Akomi
    from proceeding. Each card includes a chip explaining what needs unblocking
    (e.g. "Benefits issue", "Patient not found").
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Denied</strong> — The prior authorization was denied by the payer.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Payer Information Request</strong> — The payer issued an RFI or a
    peer-to-peer (P2P) review request.
  </li>
</ul>
